THE GALA RETREAT

Where nature, comfort,
& celebrations meet.

Gala Retreat brings together two distinctive experiences under one roof — a serene Farmhouse for relaxing getaways and intimate gatherings, and an elegant Convention Hall for grand celebrations and events.

Whether you're looking to unwind with loved ones, host a wedding, celebrate a milestone, or bring people together for a special occasion, every space is thoughtfully designed to make your experience effortless and memorable.

Stay. Celebrate. Connect. — All at Gala Retreat.

THE FARMHOUSE

A serene private retreat
where comfort meets nature.

Surrounded by lush greenery and open spaces, Gala Retreat's farmhouse offers a peaceful escape for families and friends to relax, reconnect, and celebrate together.

With spacious bedrooms, inviting living areas, a swimming pool, beautiful gardens, and thoughtful amenities, every stay is designed to feel effortless and memorable. A place to slow down, celebrate, and make moments that stay with you.
